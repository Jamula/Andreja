# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The Phase 0 prototype serves prospective users, self-host operators, developers,
support seekers, and trust reviewers who need to understand Andreja's intended
boundaries without signing in or exposing product data. Audience details remain
inferred from the ratified plan until public-site research validates them.

## Product Purpose

Andreja is a user-owned personal assistant and skill platform. The public/help
site is intended to explain the product, provide versioned documentation and
support guidance, and report evidence-backed availability and trust information.
This Phase 0 artifact tests that information architecture locally; it does not
launch or advertise a product.

## Positioning

Andreja's approved direction centers human agency, user-owned data, independently
operable self-hosting, and purpose-scoped capabilities. Broad-market demand,
production readiness, and public availability are not yet established.

## Operating Context

The proposed site is a separately built and independently deployed public/help
surface. Visitors read static content and search a pre-generated index in their
browser. Authentication, tenant data, app cookies, user tokens, feedback intake,
analytics, sponsorship, commerce, and product operations are outside this
artifact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The Phase 0 prototype is local and non-public.
- The production recommendation is a vendor-neutral pre-generated static
  artifact with in-artifact search unless a measured requirement proves SSR is
  necessary.
- Canonical content remains reviewed repository content.
- Public claims require evidence, an owner, and an expiry or revalidation rule.
- Domain, trademark, license, hosting vendor, deployment, feedback, analytics,
  and sponsorship decisions remain open and separately gated.
- The public/help site must remain product-data-free and auth-independent.

## Brand Commitments

The working product name is Andreja. Trademark, domain, public brand wording, and
license choices are not cleared by this prototype. The voice is direct,
evidence-aware, and explicit about uncertainty and unavailable features.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a ratified platform plan, architecture boundaries,
development evidence, and draft governance material. It has no approved public
brand assets, testimonials, customer counts, benchmarks, pricing, availability
commitments, production website, or public-site conformance report. Future work
must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Explain boundaries before benefits.
2. Demonstrate evidence rather than borrowing trust.
3. Keep public content separate from authenticated product systems.
4. Prefer portable, reversible delivery over provider coupling.
5. State what is planned, proven, and unavailable without ambiguity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA, supported by automated checks and manual
keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, reflow, contrast, and cognitive-accessibility
review before any public launch. Localization readiness must not depend on
English-only layout assumptions.
