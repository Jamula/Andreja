## Choose the right kind of feedback

When separately activated and evidenced:

- **Public website/help feedback** is account-free, private-first and tenant-less.
  It must not access your app cookies, tokens or user data.
- **Signed-in app feedback** authorizes only your permitted scope. It does not
  automatically include tasks, prompts or connector content. Preview everything
  explicitly selected before submitting.
- **Repository contribution** is only for sanitized contribution-safe material.
  GitHub issues, PRs and discussions are not private support or incident channels.
- **Help** should let you read known issues, release information and safe
  troubleshooting without opening a case or collecting your search terms.
- **Vulnerability, suspected unauthorized access/disclosure or personal-data
  incident** needs a separately verified private route, never public reproduction
  or ordinary spam handling. A public security/privacy *review request* is not
  an incident report.

If a report concerns Cyrus or an actual, potential or perceived conflict for
him, only an appointed independent reviewer/qualified external counsel with
verified direct contact may receive it; ordinary intake and Cyrus-mediated
handling are not substitutes. Sarek is not qualified external counsel and this
page does not promise privilege.

## What never to submit

Do not submit passwords, tokens, cookies, authorization headers, recovery or
tracking secrets; names/contact/tenant identifiers in case text; precise
locations; task/file/prompt/assistant/email/message/calendar/connector/skill
payloads; family, relationship, health, finance, employment, benefits or other
sensitive context; raw logs/traces, screenshots, archives or database exports.
Attachments are disabled unless separately approved.

Use a short sanitized symptom, affected product version/surface, expected and
actual outcome, minimal reproduction and impact/workaround. Do not attach
diagnostics to a public post to work around a blocked form. If unsafe content
was submitted or published, stop repeating it and use only an actually verified
private incident route when one is available.

## Preview and granular consent

Diagnostics default **off**. Initial candidates in the framework are product
version, client surface, occurrence time, safe correlation ID and bounded error
code; this page does not enable any of them. Each enabled field must explain
its purpose, classification and exact/redacted value preview, and require an
independent affirmative selection. A general “send diagnostics” checkbox,
silence or contact preference is not consent.

Optional GitHub publication has a **different** consent. You must see the exact
title, body, labels, acceptance evidence, safe source reference, target repository
and visibility warning. A material change requires a new preview/confirmation.
You may decline publication and still use the private workflow. Publication
can expose content to collaborators now and the public if visibility changes.
Withdrawal before publication stops it; afterward Andreja can seek removal/
redaction of controlled content but cannot promise recall of notifications,
caches, indexes, clones, forks or other people's copies.

Diagnostic withdrawal stops that optional use and follows approved deletion/
propagation rules; publication withdrawal follows its own rule. Neither
diagnostic consent nor a verified email address authorizes publication.

## Tracking, status, lost receipts and safe return channels

A display tracking reference is not a password and reveals no case existence
by itself. When implemented, status needs the separate secret or approved
verified-return proof. Keep a once-shown secret under your own safe custody;
do not paste it into URLs, issues, screenshots, email or support messages.
The service stores only a one-way verifier and cannot retrieve or resend the
original secret.

Safe status should show only coarse progress, last safe update, minimum
information requested, public help/release evidence and permitted verify/reopen
actions. It must work without repository access and must not reveal internal
issues, reporters, counts, owners or incident details. A processing acknowledgment
or receipt is not proof that an ordinary case was accepted.

If you lose a secret, use only the approved recovery proof or previously
verified return channel once available. Recovery creates a replacement and
revokes prior authority; it never reveals existence before proof succeeds.
If you have neither receipt nor verified return channel, an approved separate
private appeal must be account/receipt-free, but appeal itself cannot grant
status/access/recovery. This page offers no active appeal or non-web alternative.
Do not improvise identity proof through public posts.

You may choose no follow-up. Any future email is optional, outbound-only,
minimal and revocable; it is not marketing, reminders, a general support inbox
or the only status/rights route. Verified-channel changes require proof.
Revocation, bounce, block or complaint suppresses applicable sends; provider
failure is not permission to reveal content or contact another recipient.

## Access, correction, deletion, objection, withdrawal and appeal

The planned private rights route covers submitters **and non-user subjects**:
people described in a report who did not submit it. It must not require an
Andreja/GitHub account, submitter identity, tracking receipt or existing return
channel from a non-user subject. It uses the least intrusive sufficient
identity/relationship proof; do not send government-ID images by default.
An acknowledgment must not reveal whether a record exists.

You can request access to entitled fields, correction, deletion, objection or
withdrawal of optional consent. Proof is proportionate to the action; knowing a
reference or fragments of a report alone is not disclosure authority. Results
must exclude other people and protected/security material. Conflicting claims,
unsafe disclosure and holds require restricted review; the submitter does not
automatically win, nor does timeout release disputed content.

Outcomes must distinguish completed deletion/disassociation/redaction,
partial response, safe denial, scoped lawful retention, pending backup expiry
and copies outside control. Approved deletion must propagate to applicable
derived stores/delivery records, controlled public artifacts and backups;
restores must not resurrect deleted scope. Binding legal duties may constrain
an action. No universal timer-based purge or indefinite preservation is promised.

A partial/denied/delayed result must give a safe reason and private independent
appeal route without exposing another person. No active rights/appeal endpoint
or numerical response deadline is provided here; jurisdiction-specific lawful
deadlines and measured capacity require their own approved evidence.

## Delivery is not verification; reopening is allowed

Planned/InProgress describes intent, not a delivery date. Delivered should link
actual release/help/remediation evidence and limitations; email delivery is not
proof the problem is fixed. Verified requires your explicit confirmation or
documented objective acceptance evidence. Silence is never verification.

An item may truthfully close unverified only under an approved follow-up rule
and reason. Decline should explain why and what could justify reconsideration.
Duplicates keep independent status and verification. Once an authorized route
exists, you should be able to request reopen with minimal new sanitized evidence
without a new public disclosure; the owner must record accept/reject and reason.
