# Supporting Andreja and Creator Sponsorship

Andreja separates optional personal support for its individual creator from
organization-level project sponsorship.

## Individual creator support (`@cyrusjamula` — active)

[`.github/FUNDING.yml`](.github/FUNDING.yml) points solely to original author
Cyrus Jamula's personal GitHub Sponsors profile (`https://github.com/sponsors/cyrusjamula`),
as recorded in [`docs/repository-migration.md`](docs/repository-migration.md).

- Personal GitHub Sponsors contributions support `@cyrusjamula` as an individual
  creator.
- They are voluntary appreciation, not a commercial contract with `Jamula, Inc.`,
  not a hosted-service subscription, and not an organization or project
  sponsorship tier.

## Organization and project sponsorship (`Jamula, Inc.` — inactive)

Organization- and project-level sponsorship for `Jamula, Inc.` and Andreja is
**not active**. No corporate sponsor tiers, public sponsor directory, or
organization payment-processor intake are open.

Activating organization or project sponsorship requires all preconditions in
[`docs/business/sponsorship-policy.md`](docs/business/sponsorship-policy.md),
including:

1. Ratification of the sponsorship policy and financial-control reporting under
   [issue #11](https://github.com/Jamula/Andreja/issues/11) and
   [`docs/cost-model.md`](docs/cost-model.md).
2. Resolution of entity, tax, accounting, payment-processor, trademark, and
   licensing questions tracked in
   [issue #6](https://github.com/Jamula/Andreja/issues/6)
   ([`docs/legal/license-evaluation.md`](docs/legal/license-evaluation.md) and
   [`docs/legal/licensing-decision-packet.md`](docs/legal/licensing-decision-packet.md)).
3. Explicit human approval by Cyrus Jamula after security, privacy, legal, and
   FinOps review.

## Non-negotiable guardrails

Whether supporting `@cyrusjamula` personally today or participating in any future
ratified sponsorship program, sponsors and supporters receive **none** of the
following:

- **No user or personal data:** Zero access to user personal data, task or
  open-loop records, prompts, connector payloads, or non-public telemetry.
- **No targeted ads or ranking bias:** Sponsorship never enables targeted
  advertising, paid product placement, or preferential ranking in assistant
  responses, skills, or channels.
- **No roadmap veto or governance bypass:** Product priorities, architecture,
  security, privacy, and release gates remain governed by
  [`docs/plan.md`](docs/plan.md) and accepted ADRs. Support grants no roadmap
  control, private planning access, or security/privacy exception.
- **No automatic cloud or AI spend expansion:** Support never automatically
  expands the Phase 0 `$0` cloud-provisioning cap or the default-zero external
  AI/BYOK usage envelope. Any budget or spend change requires a separate,
  explicit approval from Cyrus Jamula under
  [`docs/cost-model.md`](docs/cost-model.md).
