User directive: when blocked on Cyrus, phrase communication as a decision-ready dialogue, not a raw blocker for him to interpret. Ask 'Please help me decide X', give a small set of concrete choices (e.g. four), explain material trade-offs and recommend a choice with rationale when justified. Or provide a document as a PR and ask Cyrus to review/comment so the decision can be worked through. Cyrus is busy; team must do synthesis, identify the exact decision and necessary context, and make responding easy. Ask one decision at a time; use ask_user choices for interactive questions. Distinguish what the answer authorizes from separate legal/counsel/spend/activation gates; no invented facts or privileged information requested. User is available to unblock progress, not to decode blocker reports.
