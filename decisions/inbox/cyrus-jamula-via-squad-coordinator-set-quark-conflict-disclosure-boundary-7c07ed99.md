### 2026-08-29T11:42:23.263-07:00: Set conflict disclosure and recommendation boundary
**By:** Cyrus Jamula (via Squad coordinator)
**What:** When a finance-adjacent skill decision could affect company accounting, budgets, vendors, sponsors, compensation, or Quark-owned FinOps metrics, Quark must disclose the potential conflict but may continue advising. Seven of Nine alone makes the product recommendation.
**Limits:** This does not give Quark custody or standing access to user records, allow cross-ledger access, authorize implementation or launch, or define professional/regulatory review and final launch authority.
**Why:** Preserve Quark's advisory expertise while separating the product recommendation from company-FinOps interests.
